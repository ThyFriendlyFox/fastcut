# Use cases

Each case has the same shape.
**Add** is what the user puts in. **Shape** is the choice they make. **Export** is the file they get.

## Split-screen reaction

**Add.** Drop the original clip and a reaction clip.
**Shape.** Pick the Reaction or Side by Side template. Turn sound on for one box.
**Export.** Get one video with both clips playing together.

## Pet highlight reel

**Add.** Drop 5–20 short clips and an MP3.
**Shape.** Pick Fast edits → Highlight Reel. Drag the song window to the chorus.
**Export.** Get a cut-on-the-beat reel with flashes, slides and pop-up boxes.

## Motivation edit with a voice-over

**Add.** Drop a talking clip and many short clips.
**Shape.** Pick Motivation. Set the talking clip to "Voice over clips". Click Auto-caption.
**Export.** Get a tall video with a small movie card, fast cuts and building words timed to the voice.

## Talking head with captions

**Add.** Drop one clip of yourself talking, plus optional b-roll.
**Shape.** Pick Talk + Captions. Click Auto-caption. Fix typos in the words box.
**Export.** Get a video with one word at a time on screen and jump zooms.

## Photo memories

**Add.** Drop photos and a calm song.
**Shape.** Pick Memories. Type one phrase per line.
**Export.** Get a black-and-white slideshow with slow fades and gentle words.

## Repost in another shape

**Add.** Open any project.
**Shape.** Open the size menu. Pick Tall, Square or Portrait, and a quality.
**Export.** Get the same edit at the new size.

## Make your own template

**Add.** Pick a template and add clips, a caption and motion graphics.
**Shape.** Drag boxes and graphics to new spots. Drag corners to resize them. Click "Save as my template".
**Export.** Pick the template from My templates next time. Export a file to use it on another computer.

## Brand the video

**Add.** Open any project.
**Shape.** Add a Name Tag or Follow Sticker in the Motion tab. Type a handle for the end card.
**Export.** Get a video that ends on your handle.
